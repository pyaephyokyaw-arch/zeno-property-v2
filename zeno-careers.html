Our Culture

How We Work Together

We believe good work starts with trust.
At ZENO, we give people room to think independently, share ideas, ask questions, and take responsibility for their work. Different perspectives are welcomed because better solutions often come from looking at a problem from another angle.

We work in a fast-moving industry, but moving quickly does not mean working without direction.
We value clear communication, thoughtful decisions, personal responsibility, and respect for the people we work with.
Every team member has a role in shaping ZENO, regardless of position or seniority.

WHY JOIN ZENO

Build Your Career Around What Matters to You.

We want people to grow with their work, not simply move from one position to another.
ZENO supports professional development through learning opportunities, mentorship, meaningful responsibilities, and the freedom to explore new areas of expertise.

Your career path does not have to follow a single direction. You may choose to deepen your current skills, explore another discipline, or take on greater responsibility as your experience develops.
What matters is that the more you learn, the more opportunities you get to contribute.

WHAT WE OFFER

A Workplace Built Around People.

Flexible Ways of Working

We focus on the quality of the work rather than simply the number of hours spent at a desk. Our flexible working approach gives team members room to organize their work while staying connected with their teams.

Support for Your Wellbeing

We want our people to have the support they need both inside and outside work. Our benefits are designed to support physical well-being, financial security, personal development, and a healthy working life.

Opportunities to Grow

Whether you want to become highly skilled in your current field, explore a different area, or develop towards a leadership role, ZENO provides opportunities to learn through real responsibilities, guidance, and collaboration.

Being Part of ZENO

More Than a Job Title.

Working at ZENO means taking responsibility for the work you create and the people who use it.

We look for people who are curious about how things work, willing to learn, comfortable with change, and prepared to take ownership.

  1. 01

    Curiosity

    Ask questions. Explore new possibilities. Learn from different perspectives.

  2. 02

    Integrity

    Be honest about what you know, what you do not know, and what needs to be improved.

  3. 03

    Ownership

    Take responsibility for your work, your decisions, and the outcomes you help create.

  4. 04

    Collaboration

    Good ideas become better when people build them together. We value open discussion and respect different points of view.

  5. 05

    Impact

    Our work ultimately affects real people making real property decisions. We measure our progress by the value we create for them.

Work Across Southeast Asia

Your Work Can Reach Beyond One City.

ZENO is being built with Southeast Asia in mind.
Our teams and operations connect different markets, perspectives, and communities across the region.

Whether you work from Bangkok, Yangon, Singapore, or another location where ZENO operates, you can contribute to a regional product with a local understanding.

Build With Us

Your Ideas Can Become Part of ZENO.

Property technology is changing how people discover and understand real estate.
At ZENO, team members contribute to that change through technology, research, design, operations, property expertise, marketing, and customer experience.

You do not have to wait for a senior title to make a contribution.

  • If you see a problem, you can raise it.
  • If you have an idea, you can share it.
  • If you see a better way to do something, you can help build it.
Make an Impact

Build Something People Actually Use.

ZENO exists to make property information and services easier to access across Southeast Asia.
The work we do can influence how someone finds their next home, chooses a rental, sells a property, or explores an investment opportunity.

That connection between your work and the people who use it is what makes building ZENO meaningful.
