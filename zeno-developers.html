Exclusive partnership

Becoming an exclusive Zeno developer

Exclusive partners give Zeno first digital release on a project. In return the project gets our full launch weight behind it.

1
Submit the project
Send company details, the development, unit mix, expected launch window, and target buyer profile through the form below.
2
Due diligence
We verify company registration, land title and construction permits, past delivery record, and escrow or payment protection arrangements.
3
Commercial agreement
We agree exclusivity scope and term, digital release window, commission structure, and the launch campaign plan in writing.
4
Launch
Project page build, photography and floor plan production, agent briefing, buyer alerts, and a coordinated release date.
What exclusivity gives you
Exclusive projects are limited per city and per quarter so each launch gets real attention. Requirements are the same standards we hold every listing to.
·Homepage and newsroom launch feature
·Priority placement in search for your area
·Named partnership manager
·Buyer alert campaign at release
·Elite agent briefing and showroom coverage
·Quarterly demand and pricing report
What we require. Valid company registration and construction permits, clear land title, verified completion record or escrow protection for off-plan sales, accurate pricing and availability kept current, and disclosure of all buyer-facing fees. Projects that cannot evidence these are not published.
