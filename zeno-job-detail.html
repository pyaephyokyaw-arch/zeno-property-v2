Account Executive

Sales 📍 Yangon, Myanmar 💼 Full time 📅 Jan 24, 2024

About Zeno Property

Zeno Property is ASEAN's leading proptech platform, connecting buyers, renters, investors, and agents across Thailand, Myanmar, and the wider Southeast Asian market. We combine AI-powered search with trusted, verified listings to make real estate simple and accessible.

We specialise in building innovative, scalable solutions that support cross-border business, digital transformation, and client success across the ASEAN market.

At Zeno Property, we foster a dynamic and multicultural environment where adaptability, initiative, and collaboration are highly valued. We empower our people to take ownership, drive impact, and grow together with the organisation.

About the Role

We are looking for a highly organised and proactive professional to join our growing team. This role is central to our mission of delivering exceptional service to clients and partners across the ASEAN region. You will work closely with cross-functional teams and contribute meaningfully to Zeno Property's continued growth.

You will play a central role in shaping a productive, professional, and welcoming workplace for our team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with clients, partners, and stakeholders across the ASEAN market
  • Develop and execute strategies to achieve team and company objectives
  • Coordinate internal and cross-functional teams to ensure seamless project delivery
  • Prepare and present regular reports and documentation for management review
  • Identify opportunities for growth and process improvement within your area
  • Represent Zeno Property professionally in all internal and external engagements
  • Assist with budget planning and monitoring in accordance with company policies
  • Liaise with relevant authorities and ensure compliance with local regulations

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Minimum 2–4 years of relevant professional experience in a similar role
  • Strong communication skills in English; proficiency in Thai or Burmese is a plus
  • Excellent organisational and time-management abilities with the capacity to handle multiple priorities
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and Google Workspace
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy in documentation and reporting
  • Experience working in a technology company, startup, or fast-paced multinational environment preferred
  • Familiarity with local labour law, social security, and visa administration is advantageous

What We Offer

Compensation & Benefits

  • Competitive salary aligned with market standards
  • Performance-based annual bonus and salary review
  • Annual leave and public holidays in accordance with the labour law
  • Company-provided MacBook for all on-site technology positions

Growth & Development

  • Clear career progression and advancement opportunities
  • Training support and professional development programmes
  • Opportunity to work on regional (ASEAN) projects and initiatives

Work Environment

  • International and multicultural workplace
  • Collaborative, supportive, and flat organisational structure
  • Opportunity to make a real impact and grow with the company
