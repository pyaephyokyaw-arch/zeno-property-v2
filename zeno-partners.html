How it works

From enquiry to live partnership

The same four stages apply to every track. Timelines vary with the depth of integration.

1
Enquiry
Submit the form with your company, track, and the markets you cover. A partnership manager is assigned on receipt.
2
Assessment
We verify company registration, licensing where required, service record, and how your offering fits our buyers in that market.
3
Agreement
Scope, commercial model, data handling, and service standards are agreed in writing before anything goes live.
4
Launch and review
Integration, badge and profile go live, then quarterly review of volume, buyer feedback, and service quality.
Partner standards. Every partner agrees to transparent pricing, disclosure of all buyer-facing fees, use of Zeno client data for the referred transaction only, and compliance with local licensing and consumer protection law. Partners falling below service standards are reviewed and can be removed from the programme.
