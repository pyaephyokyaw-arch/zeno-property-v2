About ZENO

Making Property Decisions Clearer.Smarter.

Property decisions involve more than finding a place to live. They involve understanding value, location, timing, risk, and the people you choose to work with.

ZENO was created to bring these elements together through technology, reliable information, and local market understanding. Starting in Myanmar and expanding across Southeast Asia, we are building a property platform that helps people search, compare, understand, and act with greater clarity.

We believe property technology should make decisions easier to understand, not make the process more complicated.
WHY ZENO

Property information should work for everyone.

Real estate markets across Southeast Asia are diverse and constantly changing. Information can be spread across different sources, property details can vary, and understanding the real market behind a listing is not always easy.

ZENO brings property discovery, market information, digital tools, and professional support into one connected experience.

Whether you are looking for a home, renting a property, selling an asset, or exploring an investment, ZENO is designed to help you understand your options before making your next move.

01/04
THE CHALLENGE IS….

Too much information. Not enough clarity.

Property decisions often require people to bring together information from multiple places.

Prices need to be compared. Locations need to be understood. Property details need to be checked. Financial considerations need to be calculated. Market conditions can also change the meaning behind a price.

ZENO approaches the problem differently.

We organize the information, provide practical tools, and connect users with property professionals so that important decisions can be made with a clearer view of the market.

02/04
WHAT WE BELIEVE

Better information leads to better decisions.

Technology can make property information easier to access. Data can make comparisons easier. AI can help people identify patterns and relevant opportunities.

But technology should support people, not replace the human side of property.

That is why ZENO combines digital tools with local knowledge and professional support. The goal is simple: give users better information, clearer choices, and a more connected property experience.

03/04
OUR APPROACH

Technology with a human perspective.

ZENO brings together three elements:

InformationProperty details, market information, and useful insights help users understand what they are considering.
IntelligenceDigital tools and AI help users search, compare, calculate, and identify opportunities based on their needs.
PeopleProperty professionals provide local knowledge and practical guidance when a digital platform alone is not enough.

Together, these create a property experience that is easier to navigate from discovery to decision.

04/04
OUR STANDARD

Trust is built into the experience.

A property platform is only useful when people can rely on the information and services around it.

ZENO is building its platform around property information, professional standards, responsible communication, and straightforward interactions.

Our Ambition is to make the property process easier to understand by reducing unnecessary complexity and giving users access to the information they need at each stage.

OUR VISION

One connected property experience across ASEAN.

Southeast Asia is made up of different countries, cities, markets, currencies, regulations, and property cultures.

ZENO sees an opportunity to connect these markets through technology while keeping the experience relevant to each local market.

Our vision is to create a regional property platform where people can discover opportunities, understand markets, connect with professionals, and manage property decisions across ASEAN.

Discover across markets.

Explore properties and opportunities across different cities and countries through one connected platform.

Understand before acting.

Access the information and tools needed to evaluate a property, its location, its price, and the market around it.

Connect with the right people.

Work with property professionals who understand the market and the specific requirements of each engagement.
