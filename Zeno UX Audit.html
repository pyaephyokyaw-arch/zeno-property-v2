UX Audit

ZENO Property website, all 16 pages

Stage: Pre-handoff · Date: 23 September 2026 · Scope: usability, visual hierarchy, consistency, SEO, semantic HTML and WCAG 2.1 AA

Ready with fixes. The site has a strong, consistent visual identity and clear page purposes. The biggest risk was accessibility: primary buttons, secondary text and body copy failed contrast, and there were no landmarks, skip link or labelled form fields. Those items are now fixed across every page.

What remains is mostly content and product work: placeholder links, one misattributed quote, and a few long pages that would benefit from in-page navigation.

Visual hierarchy

  • What draws the eye first: the H1 on every page, set large in Sora with gold emphasis. This is the right element.
  • Reading flow: overline, heading, supporting paragraph, CTA, repeated consistently. Numbered rows and hairline dividers make scanning easy.
  • Emphasis: gold is used for one accent per block, which keeps it meaningful. On a few long-copy cards, bold and gold compete; keep one.

What works well

  • A clear, confident brand system: one accent colour, two type families, consistent spacing.
  • Every page has exactly one H1 and all images have alt text.
  • Strong, repeated conversion paths to app download, contact and open roles.
  • Scroll motion is purposeful (reveals, drawn rules, sequential steps) rather than decorative.

Priority recommendations

  1. Replace every # link with a real destination (store badges, socials, "Read more"). Dead ends hurt trust and SEO. [AI-suggested]
  2. Add visible form labels on Contact, Jobs and Job detail. Accessible names are in place, but sighted users still lose context once they start typing. [AI-suggested]
  3. Remove or correct the Ben Horowitz quote and the "headquarters" photo caption so the credibility content is accurate. [AI-suggested]
  4. Add sitemap.xml, robots.txt and a 1200×630 share image to complete the SEO setup. [AI-suggested]
  5. Add in-page section navigation to Landing, About and Newsroom. [AI-suggested]
